About

Dr Ashwini J

Founder, Core Elite Wellness · Functional nutritionist & metabolic health consultant

For over 15 years I have bridged Ayurveda, modern clinical nutrition and functional health coaching, helping people, women in particular, reset their hormones and rebuild their health through food, gut care and lifestyle. I founded Core Elite Wellness in Bangalore in 2016 and have guided clients across the world online since 2021. I speak English, Hindi, Marathi and Kannada.

My work begins where most consultations end: with the reports that came back “normal”, the symptoms that never made it onto a form, and a history that goes back further than the last six months.

I don’t believe in one diet for everyone. I look for what is driving your symptoms, change one thing at a time, and measure whether it worked.

My approach

Not more advice. Better questions.

A few principles shape every plan I write. The details are different for every person; the thinking stays the same.

1

Start with the gut

Digestion decides what you absorb, how you feel after meals and how calm your system is. It is where I begin with every client.

2

Read the whole picture

Your history, symptoms and reports are read together, line by line, against the lab’s own ranges. Patterns matter more than one number.

3

Nourish before restricting

If your body is running low on something, we correct that first. Cutting foods from a depleted body usually backfires.

4

One change at a time

So we both know what actually helped. No crowded supplement shelves, no guesswork.

5

Charts you will actually follow

Short, clear and practical. Few words, simple lists, and recipe videos next to the dishes, made for real kitchens and real schedules.

6

Measure and re-check

We agree what “better” looks like at the start, then re-check. If something isn’t earning its place, it goes.

7

Hormones never work alone

Gut, liver, blood sugar, stress and sleep all talk to each other. A hormone concern is rarely just a hormone problem.

8

Rest is part of progress

Sleep, stress and recovery are part of the plan, not a break from it. A body that never recovers struggles to heal.

9

Your body, your response

A food can be healthy and still not suit you. We start with small swaps and what’s already in your kitchen, not an overnight overhaul.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need lab reports first?

Not to begin. If you have recent reports, bring them. If not, I’ll tell you which ones are worth doing, and in what order, so you don’t pay for tests you don’t need.

Is it a diet or a medicine?

Neither. It’s a food-first plan built around your history and your results. I don’t diagnose and I don’t replace your doctor; I work alongside them.

Will I need lots of supplements?

No. Food and gut come first. Supplements, if any, come later, one at a time and only when there is a reason for them.

I’m vegetarian or cook for a family. Can you work with that?

Yes. Many of my plans are vegetarian and built around a shared family kitchen and a busy schedule.

Do you work with people outside my city?

Yes, consultations are online and plans are shared digitally, with support on WhatsApp. We work with clients across India and around the world, and sessions are scheduled to suit your time zone.

How long is the program?

The core program is 16 weeks in four phases of four weeks each (Adjust, Boost, Transform, Maintain). After that you stay connected through the micro-community and weekly webinars.

How much does it cost?

The fee depends on your goals and health conditions. You will find it in Fees & terms just below, or request a callback and we’ll tell you what fits you.

One-on-one or group?

Both are available. One-on-one is fully personalised; the group adds community and accountability.
